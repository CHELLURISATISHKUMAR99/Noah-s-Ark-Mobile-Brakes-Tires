Mobile Service Shield — logo system

Noah’s Ark Mobile Brakes & Tires. Hand-built flat vector geometry: no gradients, shadows, glows, bevels, textures or raster data. Every file is a fill-only SVG with a viewBox, so it scales without thinning any line.

8 — Contrast constraints that shaped the mark

Measured WCAG ratios between the four approved colors. Two pairings are unusable and the geometry was built to avoid them.

PairRatioVerdict
Charcoal / Warm white15.91:1Safe
Charcoal / Steel gray5.66:1Safe
Charcoal / Safety orange5.25:1Safe
Warm white / Safety orange3.03:1Graphics only
Warm white / Steel gray2.81:1Avoid — never used
Steel gray / Safety orange1.08:1Never adjacent

Because orange on steel gray is effectively invisible (1.08:1), the wrench is sized to sit entirely inside the rotor’s charcoal centre — it never touches the steel gray ring. And because steel gray on warm white fails (2.81:1), the steel gray rotor ring is always inset into a charcoal disc, and the subtitle is charcoal on light backgrounds rather than gray.

9 — Incorrect use

Rendered here deliberately so the failure modes are visible, not just described.

✗ Dark lockup on a dark background
✗ Light lockup on a light background
✗ Stretched (aspect ratio broken)
✗ Rotated
✗ Full-colour shield below 24px
✗ Recoloured outside the palette
